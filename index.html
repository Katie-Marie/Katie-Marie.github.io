Native Android developer in New Zealand

I work on precision agriculture software. It runs on tablets mounted in agricultural vehicles and aircraft, and controls the spray and spread equipment they carry.

That splits into two halves. One is ordinary Android: Kotlin, Room, coroutines, and the long migration of a View Binding codebase to Jetpack Compose. The other is the wired-up half, where the tablet is plugged into rate controllers, GPS receivers and load cells over USB serial and CAN bus.
